Cereal Rye

Secale cereale

Cool-season annualUpright Growth

Description

Upright cool-season annual that grows 3–6 feet tall. Requires vernalization to head and produce seed. Produces thick ground cover when planted in the spring.

Main Benefits

  • ✓Retains phosphorus near roots
  • ✓Protects soil during winter
  • ✓Supports beneficial arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF)
  • ✓Retains residual nitrogen in the soil
  • ✓Rapid growth
  • ✓Strong weed suppression
windowFall - Early winter
RateBroadcast60–150 lbs/acre
RateDrilled40–120 lbs/acre
depth0.75–2 in
