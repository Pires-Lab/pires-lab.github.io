Our Team

Meet the researchers, students, and collaborators working in the Pires Lab.

Current Team

Carlos Pires

Faculty Advisor

Dr. Pires' research focuses on regenerative agriculture, soil health and nutrient management for building resilient and sustainable agroecossystems.

LinkedIn Profile

Karen Cossi

MSc. Student

Karen's research explores the long-term impacts of tillage practices, crop rotations and nitrogen sources on soil chemical, physical and biological indicator after 37 years of continuous soil building.

LinkedIn Profile

Prashant Bhatt

PhD. Student

Prashant’s doctoral research focuses on soil dynamic properties under varying management intensities across North Dakota. His study emphasizes the long-term benefits of minimal soil disturbance and the response of different soil series to these management practices.

LinkedIn Profile

Ellayna LaFond

MSc. Student

Ellayna's research examines the effects of land use on soil and water dynamics. Through this work, she aims to identify reliable indicators to define and predict soil health.
